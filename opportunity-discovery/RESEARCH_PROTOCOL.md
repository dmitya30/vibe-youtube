# Research protocol

Updated: 2026-08-22
Status: v1 active
Scope: verification method for creator mechanics before they influence any production decision

## 1. Core rule

A public claim is not evidence. A claim becomes usable only after it is matched against an observable channel history, public metrics, recurrence across independent authors, and at least one considered alternative explanation.

Official YouTube material is used as a source of rules, limits and terminology. It is not treated as a truthful growth strategy and must not replace the search for real author practice.

## 2. Source tiers

Tier 1, named author writing about an identifiable channel: author posts on Reddit including r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube, r/youtubers and r/SmallYTChannels; author threads on X; Indie Hackers and Hacker News posts; long form interviews and podcasts; personal blogs and newsletters of individual channels; public Telegram channels of Russian language authors; Discord and creator community posts; comment sections under outlier videos.

Tier 2, observable platform data: channel upload history, format changes over time, view counts relative to channel size, publication dates around a trajectory change, thumbnail and title revisions, playlist and series structure, description links and funnel destinations.

Tier 3, aggregated secondary material: tool vendor blogs, niche lists, course landing pages and generic advice compilations. Tier 3 may only generate hypotheses and lead to Tier 1 sources. It may never be the sole support for a ledger entry.

## 3. Evidence classification

Every statement in the ledger and in the map carries one label:

- OBSERVED FACT, directly visible in a primary source or on the platform at the capture date;
- CREATOR CLAIM, an assertion by an author about their own results or method;
- INFERENCE, our reasoning connecting facts and claims;
- UNVERIFIED HYPOTHESIS, a plausible mechanism with no confirmation yet;
- MARKETING CLAIM, a statement produced by someone selling a course, tool, service or channel.

## 4. Required record fields

primary URL; author or channel identity; capture date; claimed result; available public confirmation; recurrence across independent authors; possible paid traffic effect; survivorship bias exposure; conflict of interest; applicability to our resources; confidence level.

Unknown values are recorded as UNKNOWN and never silently estimated.

## 5. Inclusion threshold

A case enters the ledger only when the channel is identifiable and publicly observable, the mechanic is described concretely rather than as generic advice, and at least one verifiable time marker exists.

## 6. Promotion rule

A ledger entry may be promoted into an opportunity cluster only when the mechanic recurs across at least two independent authors and no simpler explanation is sufficient. Simpler explanations to test first: an existing audience imported from another platform; paid traffic; a single viral outlier; seasonal demand; a network or agency operating several channels; and selection of one surviving channel out of many failures.

## 7. Financial claim handling

Market size figures, income screenshots and success stories are not accepted without an explained methodology and independent confirmation. Revenue claims are recorded as CREATOR CLAIM or MARKETING CLAIM unless a public and verifiable source exists.

## 8. Measurement beyond AdSense

AdSense and YPP are not the primary business model. Scale indicators remain useful: subscribers, watch time and stable organic distribution. Funnel indicators are evaluated separately: outbound clicks, leads, bot starts, conversion rate, revenue per one thousand views and repeat purchases.

## 9. Boundaries for esoteric topics

No guaranteed predictions, no fear based pressure, no medical claims and no financial promises. Personalization stays inside an entertainment and self reflection framing.

## 10. Probe stage

Probes are designed only after mechanics survive this protocol. A probe must be cheap, fast and falsifiable, and must define its stop condition before it starts. A long program of dozens of expensive videos is not an acceptable probe design.
