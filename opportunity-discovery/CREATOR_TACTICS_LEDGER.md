Version three crossed the forty kilobyte threshold that its own rules set for archival. The
threshold exists because a ledger that cannot be read in one pass stops being working memory, and
the standing state is then reconstructed from narrative after every context compression. Versions
one to three remain valid evidence and are cited by identifier.

## CTL-0164  THE CREDENTIAL CHANNEL PAYS ONE TO TWO ORDERS MORE PER UNIT THAN THE SHELF
A first-hand operator states both figures for the same body of work: about 10000 USD from a
self-published first book, no more than 5000 USD per book once picked up by major publishers, and a
speaking honorarium typically above 30000 USD per engagement, having started at 5000 USD.
A second first-hand account from consulting reports a widely read white paper producing a string of
projects that a partner built a section of her career on.
The shelf and the credential are therefore two different revenue units for one artefact, and the
credential unit is where the money is, which quantifies the effect first observed in CTL-0157.
A recognition shortcut is reported verbatim as the mechanism: the operator is the person who
literally wrote the book on the subject, which is a positioning asset and not a sales argument.
answers: OQ-019. classification: OBSERVED FACT, capture date 2026-08-24. confidence: medium high,

## CTL-0165  CAUSALITY RUNS THE OTHER WAY AND THE CREDENTIAL PRICES A SERVICE IT CANNOT CREATE
Practitioners in the same threads state the reverse of the premise: a strong career sells a book far
more effectively than a book builds a career, and a fee is paid to a speaker whose book confirms
him rather than to an author for possessing a book.
The credential channel therefore RAISES THE PRICE of an existing service and does not bring one
into being, which is the CTL-0148 asset gap reached from the opposite direction.
Second constraint from the same source: a publisher does not market a first-time author, the
audience is built by the author during the writing, so the artefact presupposes a channel instead
of substituting for one.
Consequence: the memoir artefact of CTL-0138 cannot be evaluated as revenue on its own, only as a
multiplier on whatever the operator can already deliver or refer.
answers: OQ-019. classification: OBSERVED FACT, capture date 2026-08-24. confidence: medium high.

## CTL-0166  THE REFERRAL LOOPHOLE: THE SERVICE THE CREDENTIAL PRICES NEED NOT BE THE AUTHORS OWN
The blocking condition of CTL-0165 is ownership of a service, and a referral fee satisfies the
condition without it: the partner firm performs the work, carries the licences and the liability,
and pays a fee per signed client measured at 10000 to 26000 RUB in CTL-0136.
The memoir artefact then certifies the author as someone who WENT THROUGH the process rather than
as someone who provides it, which is the only observed configuration where the credential channel
operates without owning a deliverable.
This explains the incumbent measured in CTL-0135, who sells no file and routes viewers to a free
consultation: the monetised unit is the referral, not the shelf.
It also removes the advertising exposure of CTL-0140, because an account of personal experience is
informational content and carries no purchase call.
Open risk: a referral fee is agency income and its compatibility with the NPD regime is unsettled
as OQ-020, which must be closed before this configuration is planned.
answers: OQ-019. classification: DERIVED SYNTHESIS from CTL-0136, CTL-0138 and CTL-0165.
confidence: medium, no operator has yet been observed running this exact combination.

## CTL-0167  THE TWO REVENUE UNITS DEMAND OPPOSITE VOLUMES AND ONLY ONE FITS THE COLD START
On the owned rail of CTL-0159 a shelf artefact needs 16 to 17 sales per month to clear cost, while
a referral at about 20000 RUB net clears the same cost with roughly one signed client per month and
a single 30000 USD engagement clears a year of it.
A cold-start channel producing at most two videos per week cannot plausibly generate 17 monthly
purchases in its first months, but a single qualified referral per month is within reach of the
traffic measured on young channels in CTL-0144.
Standing rule: for a cold start the credential unit is the primary target and the shelf artefact is
a secondary by-product, which inverts the priority the workstream has carried since CTL-0097.
The shelf artefact is retained because it is what makes the credential legible and is produced as a
by-product of the pipeline per CTL-0150.
answers: OQ-019. classification: DERIVED CALCULATION, capture date 2026-08-24. confidence: medium,
the conversion rate from views to a signed referral remains unmeasured.
