## 1. Why v5 exists

Version four crossed the forty kilobyte threshold that its own rules set for archival, measured at
40407 bytes locally on 2026-08-25. The threshold exists because a ledger that cannot be read in one
pass stops being working memory, and the standing state is then reconstructed from narrative after
every context compression. Versions one to four remain valid evidence and are cited by identifier.
Size is read from the local file rather than from a remote listing, because a compressed transport
size understates the file and cannot drive the threshold.

## CTL-0201  THE SEO SURFACE IS AN ARCANUM BY SPHERE PAGE GRID, NOT A CALCULATOR GRID
SUPERSEDES CTL-0194 in part: grid breadth remains the growth lever but the grid is made of CONTENT
pages, not of calculators.
The leading Russian matrix calculator site runs only FOUR calculators, the full matrix, compatibility,
the child matrix and a health card, yet its indexed surface is a blog of arcanum by sphere pages, seen
directly in URLs of the form 11-arkan-v-otnosheniyah dated 2026-06-24.
With 22 arcana crossed by spheres such as relationships, money, health, profession and compatibility
the same four calculators generate hundreds of landing pages, and the agency that rebuilt the platform
describes exactly this work as landing pages per cluster plus FAQ and expert content.
Consequence: adding calculators is the expensive way to widen the surface, crossing the existing
calculation with life spheres is the cheap way.
