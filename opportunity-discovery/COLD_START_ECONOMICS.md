# Cold start economics

Updated: 2026-08-22
Status: first decomposition
Purpose: establish whether reaching the start of monetization from zero is arithmetically possible, and within which bounds, before any production is authorized.

## 1. Why this file exists

Public disclosure about channel growth is systematically produced by people who sell instruction rather than by people who operate channels, because publication is the funnel of the former and a cost to the latter. See CTL-0001, CTL-0024 and CTL-0027. The response is to stop verifying statements and to compute the model from observable artifacts and stated assumptions, marking every input as measured or assumed.

## 2. Advertising path, English language audience

The watch hour threshold is easier than it appears and the subscriber threshold binds first. Four thousand qualified hours equal 240000 retained minutes. At a ten minute runtime and forty percent retention this is roughly sixty thousand views. One thousand subscribers at a subscriber conversion of 0.5 to 2 percent of views, which is an ASSUMPTION and not measured, requires fifty thousand to two hundred thousand views. This confirms CTL-0007 arithmetically rather than by testimony.

Revenue at that milestone is small. One hundred thousand cumulative views at a niche RPM near 6.9 USD, a Tier 3 unverified figure, yields roughly 690 USD in total, not per month, and a mixed geography realistically yields two to four hundred USD. The honest description of the first milestone is therefore a few hundred dollars accumulated across one to two hundred thousand views.

Replacement level income is a different object. Three to four thousand USD per month requires roughly half a million views per month, which at the nine thousand view median measured in CTL-0028 requires about sixty videos per month. The observed network publishes at exactly that rate. The claim is therefore not fabricated, but the omission of the required cadence makes it misleading.

## 3. Product path

Product revenue starts far earlier than advertising revenue. Ten thousand views produce ten to seventy USD of advertising. The same ten thousand views at a link click through of one percent and a purchase rate of three percent, both ASSUMPTIONS, produce three sales, which at a 37 USD price exceeds the advertising figure and at a high price exceeds it by an order of magnitude.

This is the structural argument for C13 and it does not depend on any operator being truthful.

## 4. Russian language contour

Advertising is unavailable, see CTL-0022. The reach baseline must be read as corrected in CTL-0030: the median near 2300 views per horizontal video describes channels that were already active for a year before the slowdown, so for a new channel it is a segment ceiling rather than a starting point.

At 2300 views, one percent click through and five percent purchase, both ASSUMPTIONS, the result is approximately one sale per video. A cheap tripwire therefore cannot fund this contour at any realistic cadence. The contour requires a high ticket offer or a recurring subscription, or a shift of the input surface to Shorts, whose median is reported higher and rising.

Rising publication volume against falling reach, 556493 publications in December 2025 per CTL-0030, means the many cheap attempts strategy costs more in this market than in English language markets.

## 5. Verdict on the honesty question

The recurring distortion is a substitution of milestones. First revenue is presented as replacement income, and the industrial cadence required for the latter is omitted. Both milestones are real and the distance between them is roughly two orders of magnitude of monthly views.

## 6. Inputs that must be replaced by measurement

Subscriber conversion per view, external link click through, purchase rate, and niche RPM are currently assumptions. Each is cheap to measure on our own funnel once any probe runs, and no probe is authorized yet.
