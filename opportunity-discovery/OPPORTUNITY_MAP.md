## 1. How to read this file

This file states what is currently believed and nothing else. It is rewritten in place and is never appended to per pass. Reading order after any context compression is AGENTS.md, then README.md, then this file, then OPEN_QUESTIONS.md, and only then the ledger, and only for the identifiers those files name.

## 2. Objective and scope

A new channel is taken from zero to the start of monetization. The niche is open and is to be selected by evidence. Esotericism is one candidate. The owner products are a supplementary monetization layer for whichever channel is chosen and are never the object of investigation. Improving mature channels is out of scope.

## 3. Standing constraints

YPP entry thresholds for new applicants double on 2027-02-01 to 8000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days; current thresholds are 1000 subscribers with 4000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 1000 subscribers with 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days, and any scale target must name the version it means. See CTL-0006.

Monetization policy prohibits templates without substantive variation rather than templates as such, permits a shared intro and outro and a recurring cast across episodes when each episode differs in substance, and restricts AI personas only on the enumerated topics of health, legal issues, finances and politics. Compliance is judged at channel level. See CTL-0061.

A recurring visual frame is admissible only when each episode carries substance that would remain different if the frame were removed. If the substance survives only as a parameter change, the format is inadmissible. This is the acceptance test for any proposed format. See CTL-0063 and CTL-0061.

Videos without speech are ineligible for automatic dubbing, so removing narration forfeits multilingual reach. English as the source language yields the Russian speaking audience through dubbing while Russian does not yield the reverse. See CTL-0031 and CTL-0053.

Production capacity is one video every two to four days by hand, up to about ten per day if automated. Automation is accepted as the growth strategy in the sense of a fixed cadence held without daily discipline, not as volume scaling. A format is admissible only if its economics converge at roughly two videos per week. See CTL-0045.

No guaranteed predictions, no fear based pressure, no medical claims and no financial promises. See RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md section 9 and CTL-0044.

## 4. What is settled

Sources are systematically biased. People who operate channels do not disclose mechanics, people who sell instruction do, and the disclosure is the funnel. Verification is therefore by measurement of observable artifacts. See CTL-0001, CTL-0024 and CTL-0027.

The public channel feed at youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml supplies channel creation date, publication dates and per video view counts without authentication, which defeats the sign in wall. This is the standard measurement instrument. See CTL-0054.

Outlier to median view ratios are not stable and vary threefold for the same operator in the same week. Planning uses the median and treats outliers as a bonus. See CTL-0033 and owner confirmation.

Shorts convert to subscribers at roughly 0.14 percent and are a reach surface only. A 174 million view channel currently runs at traffic and revenue that would not fund production. See CTL-0037 and CTL-0058.

Volume is not a route. The industrial reference channel returns market median reach of about 2300 views on a fresh long form against 2.5 million subscribers, because its material is dated. See CTL-0057 and CTL-0030.

Evergreen instructional material compounds and survives interruption, and its time constant is years rather than months. Subscribers per video is a time blind metric and is withdrawn in favour of subscribers per month at a stated cadence. See CTL-0055 and CTL-0056.

Monetization within one to three months of a cold start is reported repeatedly by operators in a forum gated on monetization, including one case at three videos per month. This is testimony, recorded as a bound on what is possible and not as a plan. See CTL-0062.

Eligibility for the partner programme follows operator residency and not viewer location, while external payment services accept payment from most countries, so audience geography constrains the payment layer rather than the audience layer. See CTL-0052.

## 7. Process failure recorded on 2026-08-23

Thirteen passes ran before r/PartneredYoutube was sampled, although RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md section 2 has listed it as a Tier 1 source since the first pass, and although the same section forbids Tier 3 material from being the sole support for an entry. Two related drifts occurred in the twelfth and thirteenth passes: the sample narrowed to a single niche although the niche is open, and the reasoning began to treat the owner products as the object of investigation although the channel is the object. The structural cause was that the ledger had become unreadable in one pass and no file held the queue of open questions, so after each compression the standing state was reconstructed from narrative rather than from state. The remedies are the archive boundary, the rewritten map and OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.
