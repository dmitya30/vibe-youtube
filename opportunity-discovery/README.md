## 2. Objective

Before another long series of expensive in house experiments, extract mechanics that successful YouTube authors already use and disclose in public, verify those disclosures against observable evidence, and convert only the surviving mechanics into cheap and fast probes.

A permitted mechanic is an underused and allowed asymmetry: underserved demand, weak competitor packaging, a new or underrated format, demand transfer between platforms, search or recommendation arbitrage, a production cost advantage, a serial content construction, an unusual funnel, or external monetization.

Never recorded as an opportunity: policy violation, purchased engagement, fake identity, stolen or reused content, misleading claims, KYC evasion, spam and metric manipulation.

## 3. Deliverable definition

The first substantive result must answer:

1. where successful authors publicly disclose mechanics that actually work;
2. which mechanics are supported by observable data and not only by words;
3. which mechanics recur across independent authors;
4. which mechanics fit our resources and external monetization;
5. which three to five opportunity clusters deserve deeper research;
6. which data will later be required from the owner.
