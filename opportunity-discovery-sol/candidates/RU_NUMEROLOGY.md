# Candidate Dossier — Russian Numerology and Esotericism

Updated: 2026-08-22
Status: RESEARCH CANDIDATE / PRODUCTION NOT AUTHORIZED

## 1. Candidate thesis

A Russian-language channel may have an external-monetization advantage if a video can lead to a genuinely useful personalized result through an existing Telegram, site or bot flow.

Working path:

`YouTube → Telegram/site/bot → useful personalized result → product/subscription/service`

The existence, behavior and economics of owner products are not assumed. They will be inspected only from specifically linked files and folders when the research reaches the product-fit or integration gate.

## 2. Questions to verify

### Demand

- Which recurring Russian-language questions attract stable viewing rather than isolated viral traffic?
- Is demand informational, entertaining, identity-based, ritualized or transaction-oriented?
- Which topics produce adjacent episode depth and returning behavior?

### Competition

- Are leading results outdated, weakly packaged, overly long, generic or unsupported?
- Which formats recur among independent successful channels?
- How concentrated are views in a few legacy channels or isolated outliers?

### Creator disclosures

- Do operators publicly discuss traffic sources, format changes, Telegram transitions or product conversion?
- Can their statements be matched to upload history and public metrics?
- Are claims made while selling courses, readings, software or channel services?

### Funnel fit

- Does a viewer receive immediate standalone value before any purchase request?
- Can outbound clicks, Telegram joins, bot starts and completed results be attributed ethically?
- Is there a credible reason for repeat use or repeat purchase?

## 3. Safety and trust boundaries

The candidate must not rely on:

- guaranteed predictions or guaranteed life outcomes;
- fear, curses, threats or manufactured urgency;
- medical diagnosis or treatment claims;
- financial, investment or debt promises;
- claims of certainty about relationships, pregnancy, death or legal outcomes;
- purchased engagement, spam, fake identity or misleading testimonials;
- stolen, minimally transformed or mass-reused content.

Content should clearly frame symbolic systems as reflective, cultural or entertainment-oriented where appropriate and must not replace qualified professional advice.

## 4. Evidence required before probe design

1. A mapped set of Russian-language channels with comparable audience promises.
2. Relative outliers measured against each channel baseline.
3. At least two independent creator disclosures for any proposed tactic when available.
4. Evidence separating organic distribution from paid traffic and external celebrity effects.
5. A format-cost estimate using transferable production assets.
6. A plausible, inspectable external value path without invented product capabilities.
7. A falsification criterion that can be tested without a long expensive video series.

## 5. Candidate metrics

YouTube scale indicators:

- impressions and traffic-source mix;
- CTR and views from impressions;
- average view duration and percentage viewed;
- returning viewers and subscribers;
- organic recurrence across several uploads.

External-business indicators:

- outbound click-through rate;
- Telegram joins or bot starts per 1,000 views;
- completed personalized results;
- lead and purchase conversion rates;
- revenue and contribution margin per 1,000 views;
- repeat-use and repeat-purchase rates.

## 6. Owner-data gate

No owner product details are requested during broad market mapping. When product fit, conversion path or integration becomes the active question, request links to the exact relevant files and folders in the separate repository, plus definitions of available aggregate funnel events. Do not request a reconstructed verbal description when inspectable sources exist.

## 7. Decision boundary

Research may compare channels, formats, creator disclosures, public metrics and policy risks. It may later propose a cheap falsification probe. It does not authorize creating a channel, producing a video, publishing content or changing an existing product.
