## 3. Locked YouTube description

Turning off notifications can remove an interruption. But it may not stop you from checking your phone anyway.

In this video, we separate two different exits from a task: the door your phone opens with a notification, and the door you open yourself when work becomes unclear, uncomfortable or briefly boring.

You will also learn the Silent Door Test — a practical 25-minute experiment designed to make automatic checking visible without treating every phone use as a problem.

The goal is not to make your phone unusable. It is to make checking require a decision again.
