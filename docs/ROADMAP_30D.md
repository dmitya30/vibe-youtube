## 2. Output objective

- minimum: 4 total, including VIDEO-001;
- target: 6 total;

The stretch target is allowed only if production time falls without weakening
research, claims review, packaging, playback QA or originality.

VIDEO-003 should follow approximately 4–6 days later. Cadence after VIDEO-003
depends on measured production cost and quality defects.

## 3. Quantitative growth objective

Primary stretch milestone:

- 1,000 subscribers;
- 4,000 valid public long-form watch hours.

This is the full advertising-revenue eligibility threshold, not a forecast.
Shorts-feed watch time does not count toward the 4,000-hour route.

The expanded-YPP threshold of 500 subscribers, three valid public uploads and
3,000 public watch hours is country-dependent and does not represent full ad
revenue sharing. Russian creators currently cannot complete a new YPP signup
through a Russian AdSense account. No identity, KYC, residency or payment
restrictions may be bypassed.

## 5. VIDEO-002 decision gate

The previously discussed phone/distraction continuation is only a candidate
hypothesis. No VIDEO-002 topic, promise, title or thumbnail is locked yet.

Topic research must produce:

1. three to five concrete angles;
2. observable audience demand;
3. competitor and packaging evidence;
4. a distinct viewer problem and promise;
5. claim and sourcing risks;
6. fit with the Fewer Exits channel;
7. a recommendation with rejected alternatives.

## 6. Quality stop conditions

Slow down or stop the cadence if any of the following occurs:

- claims cannot be sourced adequately;
- the video substantially repeats VIDEO-001;
- the opening promise and delivery do not match;
- synthetic assets create material character or narrative inconsistency;
- captions, audio, playback or publication QA fails;
- production becomes template-driven with minimal substantive variation;
- automation removes required human editorial approval;
- multiple unresolved defects are carried into the next video.

Relevant monetization policy:

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en

## 7. VIDEO-001 monitoring boundary

Known early traffic includes owner-associated and personally seeded viewers.
Its exact amount cannot be separated reliably from organic traffic.

Record:

- `KNOWN_SEEDED_TRAFFIC_PRESENT=YES`
- `KNOWN_OWNER_ASSOCIATED_VIEWS=NOT_QUANTIFIABLE`
- `EARLY_SAMPLE=CONTAMINATED_FOR_ORGANIC_INFERENCE`

Do not manufacture repeat views or ask contacts to replay the video for
metrics. Early counts are observations, not evidence of product-market fit.

The 72-hour snapshot should include impressions, CTR, views, watch time,
average view duration, average percentage viewed, retention, subscribers,
traffic sources, geography and available search terms.

## 8. Localization state

- channel automatic dubbing: disabled;
- all existing experimental auto-dub audio tracks: deleted;
- all experimental translated titles and descriptions: deleted;
- reviewed English captions: retained;
- localization: deferred backlog.

No automatic translation or dubbing should be published without human review.
