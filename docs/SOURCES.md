## Source rules

Приоритет:

1. Official platform documentation.
2. Public channel/video pages.
3. Official affiliate terms.
4. Government/legal sources.
5. Peer-reviewed research.
6. Independent reporting.
7. Analytics services for orientation only.
8. Guru claims only as hypotheses.

SocialBlade, vidIQ и аналогичные оценки не доказывают реальный доход.
