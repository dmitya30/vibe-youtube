## 2. Human-QA observations

- C01 is spatially continuous and its causal sequence can be understood.
- Object motion and the project to substitutes to relief to NOW and LATER structure are useful structural evidence.
- C01 nevertheless feels monotonous rather than spectacular or strongly retentive.
- Enlarged text in the central box wraps poorly.
- The serif typography is inconsistent with VIDEO-001 through VIDEO-003.
- The beige, graphite and orange system is too flat when extended across the complete video.
- C02 through C08 resemble one long presentation because the visual modes do not contrast enough.
- The accepted Aoede narration becomes subjectively sleepy after approximately two to three minutes.
- These observations reject the current visual grammar, not the locked narration, captions, timing or causal architecture.

## 3. Owner-accepted decisions for VIDEO-004

- Do not regenerate or replace the accepted VIDEO-004 narration master.
- Keep the locked caption and scene timing manifest.
- Preserve useful structural and object-motion logic from the layout candidate.
- Redesign the visual grammar and visual rhythm before production acceptance.
- Restore a sans-serif system consistent with VIDEO-001 through VIDEO-003.
- Expand the palette with semantic accent roles and stronger light, dark and depth contrast.
- Treat wireframe people as structural placeholders only.
- Replace required character placeholders with canonical NOD keyframes from GPT Image or controlled motion from Kling after structural approval.
- Require real generative NOD motion for the C01 hero treatment.
- Evaluate sparse semantic sound and voice effects layered in Remotion without changing the narration master.
- Keep orange as a primary identity accent without limiting the complete video to three flat colors.

## 4. Owner-accepted direction for VIDEO-005 and later

- Do not select narration primarily by narrator gender.
- Test voices with stronger controlled contrast between neutral explanation, tension, turn, relief and finalization.
- Aoede must not remain the automatic default voice.
- Changing voices between successive videos is allowed as an exploratory production experiment.
- Cross-video comparisons are observational because topic, script and visual treatment also change.
- Two-speaker narration, dialogue or staged narration is allowed when designed from the script and storyboard stage.
- Do not mechanically split an already completed monologue between two voices.
- Prefer inline audio tags and prompt controls before adding another mandatory narration segment.

## 7. Visual redesign hypotheses

- Change visual modes at semantic turns rather than adding random motion.
- Give adjacent compositions distinguishable visual states.
- Use generated NOD footage, deterministic diagrams, typographic impact states, inverse dark states and depth changes as complementary modes.
- Use additional colors as semantic signals rather than decoration.
- Repair the central-box typography with controlled line breaks, bounded font sizes and explicit one-line, two-line and three-line presets.
- Preserve the C01 continuous micro-story while replacing its flat final design.
- Design the C01 generative-motion ending as a deliberate transition rather than an arbitrary loop.
